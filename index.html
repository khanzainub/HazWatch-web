flagOur Mission

To empower citizens to safely report and map hazards through an accessible geospatial platform that enables real-time disaster awareness, community participation, scientific research, and informed decision-making.

visibilityOur Vision

To create a safer and more resilient India by transforming citizens into contributors of geospatial disaster intelligence, enabling cost-effective hazard data collection, scientific research, and informed decision-making.

Citizen Science · Geospatial Intelligence

HazWatch.

HazWatch is a citizen science-based geospatial mobile application for multi-hazard incident reporting in India. It connects citizen observations with mapped hazard evidence, public analytics, and research-oriented data access.
